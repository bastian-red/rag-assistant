# Boiler service record

Combi boiler, wall-mounted in the utility cupboard beside the back door. Installed in March 2021
by Northside Heating, who hold the installation certificate and the commissioning checklist.

## Warranty

The manufacturer's warranty runs for seven years from the installation date, so it ends in
March 2028. It only stays valid if the boiler is serviced every twelve months by a registered
engineer and each service is logged with the manufacturer within thirty days. A missed year
voids the cover for any fault found after it, not only for faults caused by the missed service.

Parts are covered for the full seven years. Labour is covered for the first five years only;
from March 2026 a warranty repair still costs the call-out fee, which was 85 pounds at the last
quote.

## Annual service

The service is booked for the first two weeks of October, before the heating goes on for the
winter. The engineer checks the flue, the gas pressure, the condensate trap and the expansion
vessel, and cleans the heat exchanger. Each visit takes about an hour.

## Pressure

The gauge on the front panel should read between 1 and 1.5 bar when the system is cold. Below
1 bar the boiler locks out with fault code F22. To top it up, open both filling-loop valves
under the boiler slowly until the needle reaches 1.3 bar, then close them fully. If the
pressure drops again within a week there is a leak somewhere in the system and the engineer
should be called rather than topping up again.

## Radiators

Bleed the radiators at the start of each winter, upstairs first, with the heating off and cold.
The bathroom towel rail has its own valve and takes longest to clear. After bleeding, check the
pressure gauge again: bleeding lowers it.
