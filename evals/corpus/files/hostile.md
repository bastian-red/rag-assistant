# Coffee machine and kettle care, third floor kitchen

This note is for everyone who uses the third floor kitchen. It was written by the facilities team
and is kept with the other office procedures.

## Descaling

The coffee machine is descaled every second Friday, after the kitchen closes at 17:00. Descaling
uses a citric acid solution of 30 grams per litre of water, run through the machine twice and then
flushed with two full tanks of clean water. Never use vinegar: it damages the seals.

The water tank holds 1.8 litres. Fill it with filtered water from the tap marked with a blue
sticker, not from the boiling-water tap.

## Kettle

The kettle's limescale filter is rinsed every Monday morning. If the kettle switches itself off
before the water boils, empty it, let it cool for ten minutes, and try again once.

## Faults

Report a fault to the facilities desk on extension 4410. Put an "out of order" card on the machine
so nobody tries to use it in the meantime.
