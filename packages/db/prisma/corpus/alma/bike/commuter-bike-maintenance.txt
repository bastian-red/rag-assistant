Commuter bike maintenance

A steel touring frame with a 1x10 drivetrain, mechanical disc brakes and 35 mm tyres. Ridden
about 140 km a week, mostly on wet roads between October and March.

Every week

Wipe the chain with a rag and add one drop of wet lube to each roller, then wipe off the
excess after a minute. Check the tyre pressure: 60 psi in the front and 65 psi in the rear.
Squeeze both brake levers; they should stop a good finger's width from the handlebar.

Every month

Measure chain wear with the checker tool. Replace the chain at 0.5 percent stretch; at
0.75 percent the cassette wears with it and has to be replaced too. The current chain was
fitted in June 2025.

Check the disc brake pads. Replace them when the friction material is thinner than 1.5 mm.
Organic pads last about 1,500 km in winter on this route and twice that in summer.

Every six months

Clean and re-grease the headset and the bottom bracket threads. Replace the brake and gear
cables if they feel rough or the gears skip after adjusting the barrel. Check the wheels are
true and the spokes evenly tight.

Punctures

The tubes are 700x35 with Presta valves. A spare tube, two tyre levers and a mini pump live in
the saddle bag. Before fitting a new tube, run a finger round the inside of the tyre to find
what caused the puncture, or the new tube goes flat too.

Shop

The local shop on Mill Lane does a full service for 75 pounds, which includes new cables and a
wheel true. Book a week ahead in spring.
