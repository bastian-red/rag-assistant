# Flat lease: the parts that matter

A plain summary of the tenancy agreement for flat 4, 18 Calder Road, written for my own
reference. The signed agreement is the one that counts if this summary and it ever disagree.

## Term and rent

The tenancy is an assured shorthold for twenty-four months, from 1 September 2025 to
31 August 2027. Rent is 1,150 pounds a month, due on the first of each month by standing order.
The landlord may raise the rent once, on the first anniversary, by no more than the change in
the consumer price index over the previous year, with at least two months' written notice.

## Deposit

The deposit is 1,325 pounds, held in a government-approved deposit protection scheme. The
scheme's certificate arrived by email on 9 September 2025. At the end of the tenancy the
landlord has ten days to propose any deductions, and anything disputed goes to the scheme's
free adjudication instead of being kept.

## Break clause

Either side may end the tenancy early from month twelve onwards with two months' written
notice. The earliest end date under the break clause is therefore 31 August 2026 if notice is
given by 30 June 2026. Notice has to be in writing; an email to the managing agent counts.

## Repairs

The landlord is responsible for the structure, the exterior, the boiler, the water heating and
the wiring. The tenant is responsible for small things: light bulbs, smoke alarm batteries,
blocked sinks caused by use. Anything urgent, such as a leak or no heating, is reported to the
managing agent's emergency line, which answers at any hour. Non-urgent repairs are reported by
email and should be acknowledged within five working days.

## Pets and subletting

One cat is allowed by name in the agreement. Subletting any part of the flat is not allowed,
and neither is running a business that brings visitors to the building.

## Leaving

At the end of the tenancy the flat has to be returned in the condition recorded in the
inventory of 1 September 2025, allowing for fair wear and tear. The carpets are to be
professionally cleaned only if they were cleaned before move-in, which the inventory says they
were.
